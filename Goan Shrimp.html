Goan Shrimp Curry

Description

Goan shrimp curry is a rich and flavorful coastal dish made with fresh prawns, coconut, and aromatic spices. It has a perfect balance of tangy and spicy flavors, making it a comforting and delicious meal.

Ingredients

  • 250g shrimp
  • 1 cup grated coconut
  • 2 dried red chilies
  • 1 small onion ;5 garlic cloves
  • 1 tsp turmeric powder
  • 1 tsp red chili powder
  • 1 tbsp tamarind pulp
  • 1 tbsp oil
  • Salt to taste
  • Water as needed

Steps

  1. Grind coconut, red chilies, garlic, and a little water into a smooth paste.
  2. Heat oil in a pan and sauté the chopped onions until soft.
  3. Add turmeric powder and chili powder, then mix well.
  4. Add the ground coconut paste and cook for a few minutes.
  5. Pour in some water to adjust the consistency and bring it to a boil.
  6. Add shrimp and cook for 5–7 minutes until they are tender.
  7. Add tamarind pulp and salt, then simmer for a few more minutes.
