Fudgy Brownies

Description

Fudgy brownies are rich, dense, and chocolatey treats with a soft and gooey center. Perfect for dessert lovers, they are easy to make and incredibly satisfying with every bite.

Ingredients

  • 1/2 cup butter
  • 1 cup sugar
  • 2 eggs
  • 1 tsp vanilla extract
  • 1/3 cup cocoa powder
  • 1/2 cup all-purpose flour
  • 1/4 tsp salt
  • 1/4 tsp baking powder
  • 1/2 cup chocolate chips

Steps

  1. Preheat the oven to 180°C and grease a baking pan.
  2. Melt the butter and mix it with sugar until smooth.
  3. Add eggs and vanilla extract, then mix well.
  4. Add cocoa powder, flour, salt, and baking powder, and stir until combined.
  5. Fold in the chocolate chips.
  6. Pour the batter into the baking pan.
  7. Bake for 20–25 minutes.
  8. Let it cool, then cut into squares and serve.
