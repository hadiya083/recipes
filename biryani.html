Chicken Biryani

Description

Chicken biryani is a flavorful and aromatic rice dish made with tender chicken, fragrant basmati rice, and a blend of rich spices. It is a popular Indian dish known for its delicious taste and comforting aroma.

Ingredients

  • 500g chicken
  • 2 cups basmati rice
  • 1 large onion (sliced)
  • 2 tomatoes (chopped)
  • ginger garlic paste
  • 2 green chilies
  • 1/2 cup yogurt
  • 1 tsp turmeric powder; chilli powder; garam masala; salt to taste
  • 2 tbsp oil or ghee
  • Fresh coriander leaves and mint

Steps

  1. Wash and soak the basmati rice for 20–30 minutes.
  2. Heat oil or ghee in a pan and sauté sliced onions until golden brown.
  3. Add ginger garlic paste and green chilies, then cook for a minute.
  4. Add chopped tomatoes and cook until soft.
  5. Add turmeric, chili powder, and garam masala, then mix well.
  6. Add chicken and cook until it is partially done.
  7. Add yogurt and cook for a few more minutes.
  8. Boil rice separately until it is 70% cooked.
  9. Layer the rice over the chicken and cover the pan.Cook on low heat for 10–15 minutes.
  10. Garnish with coriander leaves and serve hot.
